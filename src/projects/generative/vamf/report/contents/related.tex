\section{Related Work}
\label{sec: review}

\paragraph{Flow Matching.} Continuous normalizing flows (CNFs)~\cite{chen2018neural,grathwohl2019ffjord} parameterize a time-dependent velocity field and integrate it to transform a base distribution, extending earlier normalizing-flow approaches~\cite{rezende2015variational,dinh2017density}. Conditional flow matching~\cite{lipman2023flowmatchinggenerativemodeling,tong2024improvinggeneralizingflowbasedgenerative} and stochastic interpolants~\cite{albergo2023buildingnormalizingflowsstochastic,albergo2025stochasticinterpolants} enable simulation-free training by exploiting the identity $\vv(\rvx,t)=\E_{\rvx_{0}\mid\rvx_{t}}\!\left[\vv(\rvx,t\mid\rvx_{0})\right]$, replacing the intractable marginal velocity with conditional samples. Minibatch optimal transport~\cite{tong2024improvinggeneralizingflowbasedgenerative,peyre2019computational} further straightens conditional paths. Scaling rectified flow transformers to high resolution~\cite{esser2024scaling} demonstrates the practical power of this paradigm. Our analysis extends the framework to the two-parameter average velocity setting, where the conditional expectation identity breaks down due to trajectory curvature (\cref{thm: material-derivative-expansion}).

\paragraph{One-Step Generation.} Reducing the number of function evaluations to one is a central goal. DDIM~\cite{song2021denoising} first enabled deterministic sampling with larger step sizes from diffusion models. Consistency models~\cite{song2023consistencymodels,song2023improvedtechniquestrainingconsistency,lu2025simplifyingstabilizingscalingcontinuoustime} enforce that all points on a PF-ODE trajectory map to the same endpoint, with extensions to trajectory consistency~\cite{kim2024consistencytrajectorymodelslearning} and easy fine-tuning~\cite{geng2024consistencymodelseasy}. Distillation-based methods~\cite{salimans2022progressivedistillationfastsampling,yin2024distributionmatchingdistillation,yin2024improveddistributionmatchingdistillation,zhou2025scoreidentitydistillation} achieve strong FIDs~\cite{heusel2017gans} on standard benchmarks~\cite{krizhevsky2009learning} but require a pre-trained teacher. InstaFlow~\cite{liu2024instaflow} combines rectified flow with distillation for text-to-image one-step generation. Flow map matching~\cite{boffi2025flowmapmatchingstochastic} and shortcut models~\cite{frans2025stepdiffusionshortcutmodels} provide principled frameworks for two-time maps. Recent work unifies consistency and flow matching~\cite{yang2024consistencyflowmatchingdefining,wu2025scotunifyingconsistencymodels}, and FACM~\cite{peng2025facmflowanchoredconsistencymodels} anchors consistency training with a flow matching loss---a mechanism related to our FM anchor, though within a different training framework.

\paragraph{MeanFlow and Variants.} MeanFlow~\cite{geng2025meanflowsonestepgenerative} learns the average velocity field via a total-derivative identity, enabling distillation-free one-step generation. AlphaFlow~\cite{zhang2025alphaflowunderstandingimprovingmeanflow} identifies gradient conflict between the flow matching and total consistency components, proposing $\alpha$-scheduling to manage the tradeoff. Improved MeanFlow~\cite{geng2025improvedmeanflowschallenges} traces instability to the stochastic JVP tangent and introduces a separate velocity head with stop-gradient. Re-MeanFlow~\cite{zhang2026overcomingcurvaturebottleneckmeanflow} addresses trajectory curvature through a reflow pre-processing step. Terminal Velocity Matching (TVM)~\cite{zhou2026terminalvelocitymatching} sidesteps the spatial Jacobian entirely by differentiating w.r.t.\ the terminal time, and Functional Mean Flows~\cite{li2025functionalmeanflowhilbert} independently establishes the marginal-conditional gap for average velocities in a Hilbert space setting. Physics-informed distillation~\cite{tee2024physicsinformeddistillationdiffusion} derives a related PDE-based loss for learning flow maps. Our curvature-variance principle unifies these diagnostics---our method addresses all failure modes without architectural changes, separate heads, or pre-training.

\paragraph{Variance Reduction in Flow Training.} Several concurrent works address variance in flow-based training. Stable Velocity~\cite{yang2026stablevelocityvarianceperspective} reveals a two-regime variance structure in flow matching and proposes composite conditioning; TPC~\cite{maduabuchi2026temporalpairconsistencyvariancereduced} reduces variance through temporal pair coupling; and preconditioned flow matching~\cite{ahamed2026preconditionedscoreflowmatching} addresses ill-conditioning via anisotropic preconditioning. Bertrand et al.~\cite{bertrand2025closedformflowmatchinggeneralization} argue that stochastic target variance is negligible for standard flow matching---consistent with our analysis, since the problematic amplification is specific to the MeanFlow JVP, not the flow matching loss itself. Rectified flows~\cite{liu2022flowstraightfastlearning,lee2024improvingrectifiedflows} reduce trajectory curvature by iterative straightening, which by~\cref{thm: material-derivative-expansion} drives $\frac{\mathrm{D}\vv}{\mathrm{D}t}\to 0$.
